Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our products, clean formulations, deliveries, and returns.

Are Luméra Beauty formulations 100% cruelty-free and vegan?
Yes, absolutely. None of our formulations or raw ingredients are ever tested on animals. All products are 100% certified cruelty-free and vegan.
How long does domestic shipping take across India?
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days across major metros and tier-2 cities. Priority Express Air courier delivers within 24 to 48 hours.
Is complimentary shipping available?
Yes! All orders valued above ₹999 qualify automatically for Free Standard Shipping throughout India.
Which coupon codes can I apply at checkout?
You can use GLOW20 for 20% OFF on orders over ₹1,500, WELCOME15 for 15% OFF for new members (orders above ₹800), and BEAUTY10 for 10% OFF any order.
Can I place an order or enquiry directly via WhatsApp?
Yes, you can directly connect with our beauty concierge at +91 9887972121 or use our dedicated WhatsApp Enquiry Page.
Are Luméra skincare serums safe for sensitive skin?
Our clinical range is formulated without parabens, phthalates, synthetic sulfates, or harsh fragrances. They are dermatologically tested for hypoallergenic tolerance.
What is your return and exchange policy?
We offer a 7-day hassle-free doorstep replacement for items received damaged, defective, or incorrect in seal.
Are Cash on Delivery (COD) and UPI accepted?
Yes! We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Visa/MasterCard credit and debit cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery.
