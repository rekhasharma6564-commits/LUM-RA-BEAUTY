Returns & Replacements

1. 7-Day Replacement Guarantee

Due to hygiene and cosmetic safety regulations, we accept replacements within 7 days of delivery in cases where an item arrives damaged, leaking, defective, or incorrect.

2. How to Request a Replacement

Simply send your Order ID along with photos of the damaged item to our official WhatsApp support at +91 9887972121 or email care@lumerabeauty.com.

3. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment source (UPI / Card / Net Banking) within 3–5 working days.
