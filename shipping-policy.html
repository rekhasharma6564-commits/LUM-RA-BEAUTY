Shipping & Delivery Policy

1. Shipping Costs & Complimentary Delivery

All orders above ₹999 qualify for Complimentary Standard Delivery across all states in India. Orders below ₹999 incur a flat nominal shipping fee of ₹99. Express Air Courier is available at ₹199.

2. Dispatch & Delivery Timelines

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours from our climate-controlled beauty distribution center. Delivery generally occurs within 3–5 business days.

3. Order Tracking

Once dispatched, you will receive a tracking link via SMS and WhatsApp. You can also track your live shipment anytime on our Track Order page.
